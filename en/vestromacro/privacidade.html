Privacy

What data Vestro Macro uses, what for and for how long. Applies to the app and to this site.

What stays only on your computer

Everything you record and build in Vestro: workflows, lists, constants and whatever the macro copies and pastes. These files are stored with Windows' own encryption and are not sent anywhere.

What the app sends

To activate the license and check from time to time that it's still valid, Vestro sends the license server:

  • the license key;
  • a computer fingerprint: a code calculated from the Windows ID and the motherboard model, which can't be used to recover that data;
  • the computer name and the Windows user name;
  • the app version.

The server also logs the IP address the check came from.

This data is used to check the license, tie the fixed license to one computer, manage the floating license's seats, approve computer changes and block abuse attempts.

The free trial

When you open Vestro without a license key, it asks the server for the 30-day free trial and sends only:

  • the computer fingerprint (the same code as above);
  • the computer name;
  • the app version.

The server keeps this data to remember that the computer has already used the trial (so it doesn't restart when you reinstall) and to know when the trial ends. During the trial, the Windows user name is not sent, and the IP address is used only at that moment, to limit abuse, without being stored. For the same purpose, only a code for the network the request came from is kept for one day, which can't be used to find out the IP.

Updates

To find out whether there's a new version, Vestro sends the server only the installed version. None of this is stored. The new version is downloaded from GitHub, where the download file is hosted.

Your account on the site

If you create an account on the site, we store the name, email and contact phone you provide, and the password only in a scrambled form (a hash), which can't be used to find out the password. The account is only created after you open the link we send to your email. You can see and change this data in the account itself. The licenses you add are linked to the account, so you can see their status.

We use your email only to send account links (confirming your email, creating a new password), through the Resend email service, which receives your address and the message text to deliver it. Each link works once and expires (in 48 hours; the new-password one, in 1 hour).

When you sign in, the site stores a single cookie in your browser, needed to keep you signed in. It doesn't track anything and lasts 30 days, or until you sign out. The IP address you signed in from is kept with the session, for security. Getting the password wrong many times locks attempts for a few minutes; the IP used for that limit is not stored. Requests that send an email (sign-up, “forgot my password” and email change) are also counted, for one day, under a code for the network they came from, which can't be used to find out the IP.

To open the site in your language and to know whether buying on the site is available, the site uses the country the visit comes from (provided by Cloudflare based on the IP) and, if it's not available, the browser's language, without storing them.

When you buy

On the site, buying requires an account: the license is created linked to it, with the account's name, email and phone. Payment is made on Mercado Pago, which receives the payment details (Pix, card, CPF) directly from you, under Mercado Pago's privacy policy. Vestro Macro doesn't see or store card details: it stores only the order (plan, amount, date and the Mercado Pago payment number) and, for automatic renewal, the Mercado Pago subscription number, its status and the date of the next charge. The license key goes to your email and is stored encrypted, so you can copy it from your account.

By email or WhatsApp, the name and contact you give us are stored with the license, to identify the license and provide support.

For how long

  • The license's check history is deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • The free trial record (computer fingerprint, computer name and dates) is kept so the same computer doesn't get another trial.
  • Links sent by email (and, at sign-up, the data you typed until opening the link) are deleted when you use the link or when it expires.
  • The account session (the cookie and the IP you signed in from) is deleted when you sign out, change your password or email, or after 30 days. The account data stays as long as the account exists.
  • An order that wasn't paid is deleted after 30 days. A paid one is kept as a record of the sale (even after the account is deleted, with no link to it).
  • License data (name, contact and the last computer that used it) stays as long as the license exists. When the license is deleted, it's erased. Only a record that it was deleted remains, with the customer's name and the end of the key, for up to 400 days.

Who the data is shared with

The data is not sold or passed on. Payment is made on Mercado Pago (see “When you buy”). The license server and this site are hosted on Cloudflare, a company that only stores and delivers the data for Vestro Macro, without using it for anything else, and that may store it outside Brazil, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Older versions of Vestro check the license by first going through a Vestro Macro server, which only passes the data on to Cloudflare.

This site doesn't use analytics or tracking, and only uses the account cookie, for those who sign in. Cloudflare may log the IP address of visitors, for security. The download file is hosted on GitHub, under the GitHub privacy statement. Conversations on WhatsApp follow WhatsApp's own policy.

Why this data can be used

For checking the license and for the account on the site, the legal basis is the performance of a contract (Brazil's General Data Protection Law, LGPD, art. 7, V). For the free trial and the limits against abuse, it's the legitimate interest in offering the trial once per computer and protecting the service (art. 7, IX).

Your rights

You can ask to know what data exists about you, and to correct or delete it (including your account on the site). Deleting the license data ends the license: Vestro keeps working, but only in restricted mode.

For any request or question, contact support by email, at contato@vestroautomation.com.
